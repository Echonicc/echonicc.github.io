I am a PhD candidate at Queen Mary University of London. My research lies at the intersection of digital finance, corporate ESG performance, climate risk, and innovation. I employ empirical methods including spatial econometrics and patent analysis to investigate how technological and financial factors shape firm behaviour and regional development.
